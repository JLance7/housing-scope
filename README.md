# HomeScope

HomeScope helps you compare places to live by what they might cost each month. Explore rentals and homes for sale in the cities you care about, starting with New York.

## What makes HomeScope different?

- **Start with monthly cost.** Compare rent with estimated homeownership costs that include mortgage payments and any available taxes, insurance, and HOA fees.
- **Search the cities you care about.** Filter by city, property type, and monthly budget. Signed-in users can add cities and request a refresh, subject to the app's shared request limits.
- **Save places privately.** Sign in with Google to keep a list of favorites tied to your account.
- **Know when a listing was checked.** Online results show when HomeScope fetched them. Demo listings are labeled.
- **Watch for listings that fit.** The site owner and approved users can enable scheduled city updates and receive email alerts for matches by budget, address keyword, or listing type after confirming the subscription.

Online listings come from RentCast when configured. Results cover a subset of available inventory, and prices or availability may change between updates. Monthly ownership costs are estimates. Verify listing details with the provider.
